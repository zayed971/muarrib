How this works & what to trust. Each page is rendered to an image and read by a vision model — so corrupted PDF text encoding never enters the pipeline. Output is a clean reflowed reading view, not a pixel copy. Tables are rebuilt as Arabic tables. Figures and charts are described in Arabic; the real graphic is shown under “Show original” — the tool never redraws a chart or invents data. Numbers, dosages, units and statistics are transcribed exactly; anything blurry or uncertain is marked غير مؤكد so you can check it against the source. For medical, legal, or research use, verify flagged passages and key figures against the original.
